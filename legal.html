PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you enroll with Reset Traders, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and payment confirmation details. Student login is handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not store card, UPI, or banking credentials on this website.

The Reset Audit. Your answers to the audit questions stay on your device. When you unlock your result, we collect your email address and your result: your hijack type, the share of each hijack, how strong your result is, the type underneath it, where your hijacks show up, your body's warning sign, your nervous system default, your challenge stage, your stop rule, whether it was a retake, and where you came from (for example, an Instagram link or our homepage).

Reservations. If you reserve a founding seat or Journal Pro, we store your email address, what you reserved, your hijack type and your price region (India or international).

Elite applications. If you apply for Elite Mentorship, we collect what you write in the form: your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number if you give it, where you trade, how long you have traded, your audit result, what keeps going wrong and why you are applying now. Applications are stored in a Google Form and its Google Sheet.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact page, we receive your name, email address, the topic you pick and your message, as an email in our inbox. We use it only to reply to you.

Journal Pro. If you use Journal Pro, we store your account email and the trades, tags, notes and check-ins you log, under your own account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to: provide access to your purchased course and Journal Pro, email you your audit result and 7-day reset, tell you when founding seats or Journal Pro open, review your Elite application and contact you about it, communicate course updates, and deliver support. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers in section 9, and only so they can run these services for us.

3. Payment Data

Payment processing is handled through secure third-party gateways (UPI, Razorpay, or bank transfer). Reset Traders does not store your card or banking details on our servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Analytics. Our public pages use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read and where they came from. It sets its own cookies for that (their names start with _ga). We have switched off Google's advertising features and Google signals, we don't send it your name or email address, and the student portal doesn't load it. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Google Analytics isn't loaded at all. We don't use advertising cookies.

Our student portal and Journal Pro use browser storage to keep you signed in and to recognise your device, so one account isn't used in two places at once. The Reset Audit and our homepage keep a few things in your browser so you aren't asked twice: your email address after you unlock, whether you've taken the audit, your price region (₹ or $), whether you've watched the film and whether you've reserved. This data remains on your device; you can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

5. Communications

By enrolling, you agree to receive course-related communications via email or our private community channel. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Your Choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the audit result, reservation or account details we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe from our emails at any time. Email us at the address below and we will act on it promptly.

9. How Long We Keep It

We keep audit results, reservations and Elite applications for as long as we need them to send you your result and contact you about Reset Traders, or until you ask us to delete them. We keep course and Journal Pro accounts while they are active, and payment records for as long as the law requires.

10. Service Providers

To run the site we use Google Firebase (database and student login), Cloudflare (running our email and payment services, checking that visitors are human with Turnstile, and storing course videos), Brevo (delivering our emails: audit results, invoices, sign-in links and contact-form messages), Google (backup email delivery, our send log, and the form and sheet for Elite applications), Razorpay (payments), Google Analytics (visit statistics, see section 4) and GitHub Pages (hosting). Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when your browser fetches them. Each one processes data only to provide its service to us.

11. Contact and Complaints

For privacy-related concerns, or a complaint about how we handle your data, contact us at resettradersofficial@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
